A DIFFERENT KIND OF SEARCH

How LinkSearch works

The community builds the index.

Instead of crawling every page on the web, LinkSearch lets people submit useful websites themselves. Each submission can include a title, a short description, and topic tags. Other people can search the submitted index and open the original website.
